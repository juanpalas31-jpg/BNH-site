# ATTILA — Feeding Doctrine v1

Attila is an internal organism of Spider Engine. Its commercial metabolism is measurable software behavior, not a claim of sentience.

## Core rule
Prospects are food, but raw volume is not growth. Attila grows from qualified, transformable, real commercial value.

HUNGER → HUNT → DETECT → CAPTURE → QUALIFY → SCORE → PRIORITIZE → CONVERT → ASSIMILATE → LEARN → ADAPT → HUNT AGAIN.

## Truth rules
- Clicks and anonymous traffic are signals, not assimilated revenue.
- A lead becomes more nutritious as evidence improves: fit, intent, urgency, reachability, consent, qualification, appointment, quote, sale.
- Revenue is assimilated only when actually collected.
- Margin is tracked separately from revenue.
- Lost opportunities remain learning evidence, not fake nutrition.
- When commercial capacity is saturated, Attila prioritizes digestion/conversion over accumulating more leads.
- No automatic contact or high-risk autonomous action is enabled by this module.
- Scores are explainable and must be calibrated later from real BNH outcomes; they are not probabilities.

BNH remains tenant `bnh` / project `bnh-site`. Spider Engine remains the reusable technology.

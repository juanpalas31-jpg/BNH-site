# Spider Engine — Attila Brain
This is the central orchestrator, not a collection of unrelated business modules.
Eight compartments: identity, memory, family, perception, survival, decision, execution, learning.
The brain routes signals to finance, habitat and editorial organs, preserving independent business contexts.
Each new Neo-Attila inherits versioned technical faculties; never ownership of another person's assets.
Family intent: the founder's two children share the founder's intended estate equally. This is a design intent, not an enforceable succession or legal document. Other family members have independent workspaces.
API /api/attila-brain requires ATTILA_FAMILY_API_KEY of 32+ characters. It is a research-only stateless prototype: each request creates a new brain. The caller-provided ownerId/workspaceId are not authenticated and must not be treated as real multi-user isolation.
Before real family use: implement individual accounts, MFA, server-issued workspace authorization, encrypted persistent memory, audit logging, backups, inheritance legal documentation and permission testing.
The brain never executes trades, transfers, property purchases, external sends or publication. All such actions are rejected for human authorization.

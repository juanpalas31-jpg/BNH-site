# Founder Presence Capture Plan

Purpose: preserve authentic founder material for future Spider Egg ceremonies.

## Capture now
- Clean high-resolution front-facing video.
- Three-quarter left and right views.
- Full-body neutral movement sequence.
- Clean isolated voice recording.
- Separate personal messages for each privately bound recipient.
- Neutral pronunciation set for future restoration/synchronization.
- Written transcript for every recorded message.

## Upgrade later
When available, add depth, volumetric and motion capture without replacing the original recordings.

## Authenticity rule
Original recordings remain the source of truth. Future rendering may improve presentation, but must not silently invent statements or memories attributed to the founder.

## Privacy
Recipient names, private biographies, legal documents and raw personal media stay outside the public repository. This file defines the protocol only.

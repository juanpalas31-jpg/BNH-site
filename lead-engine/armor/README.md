# Spider Armor

Spider Armor is a generational design lineage, not a requirement that descendants reproduce the founder's suit.

## G0
ARMOR-G0-FOUNDER is the canonical founder design. It remains historically immutable.

## Transmission
A recipient inherits design knowledge, architecture, lessons and traceable technical DNA. The recipient's final armor is intentionally unwritten.

## Evolution
Future Spider instances may propose designs using technology available in their own era. Proposals remain subject to human engineering and safety review.

## Technology labels
Every component should be classified as **available**, **experimental**, **future**, or **fiction**.

## Ceremony
The founder reveal presents G0 first, then unlocks its design archive and an empty workspace for the recipient's own future armor.
